## Overview

This is a living index of my experiments with machine learning and AI. The aim is to go beneath the interface: understand how systems are built, where they break, and what changes when an idea is implemented rather than only read about.

## Why this exists

Modern AI moves quickly. Experiments are a practical way to distinguish what a model *appears* to do from what an implementation actually supports.

## Areas of experimentation

- Implementing and understanding model architectures.
- Training and evaluation workflows.
- Inference and deployment tradeoffs.
- Language models, retrieval, and conversational interfaces.
- Reliability, constraints, and system behavior.

This page describes areas of interest; it is **not** a claim that every item is a completed project.

## Overview

Not every worthwhile engineering problem becomes a standalone product. This space is reserved for the smaller tools, prototypes, explorations, and ideas that contribute to the larger learning process.

## The kind of work that belongs here

- Useful software built to solve a specific problem.
- Interfaces and systems tested as prototypes.
- Technical experiments worth documenting.
- Explorations that did not work—and the reasons why.

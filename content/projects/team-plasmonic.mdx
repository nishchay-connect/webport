## Overview

Team Plasmonic is an interdisciplinary project investigating **H₂S detection** and the sensing principles that could make useful detection systems possible. The exploration includes plasmonic and chemoresistive approaches, connecting materials-level behavior with the engineering demands of a usable device.

## The problem

Hydrogen sulfide can present a serious exposure hazard. Detecting it reliably raises questions about sensitivity, environmental conditions, readout methods, practicality, and user interpretation.

## Motivation

The project is an opportunity to learn across boundaries: materials science, electronics, device design, and how technical choices affect human safety.

## Technical direction

Key threads to examine include:

1. **Sensing principles** — how material responses could indicate the presence of H₂S.
2. **Signal and readout** — how a physical or chemical change becomes an interpretable indication.
3. **Design constraints** — cost, reliability, operating environment, and safe use.

These are **research directions**, not assertions that a sensor has achieved particular performance.

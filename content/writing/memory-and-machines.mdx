Memory is a technical concept in computing and a deeply personal one in human life. The same word points to very different phenomena: stored information, learned patterns, recalled experience, and a sense of identity across time.

## Some open questions

What separates the storage of information from the experience of remembering? How does continuity arise when both people and machines change over time? Is a functional description enough to explain subjective life?

These questions invite ideas from neuroscience, computer science, philosophy, and cognitive science. They do not yield easy conclusions.
